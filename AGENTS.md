- Service order rules (warranty days, OS number, validation) live in src/lib/os.ts with tests in os.test.ts; PDF drawing in src/lib/pdf.ts — keep business rules out of UI.
- Offline service worker is generated by vite-plugin-pwa and registered only from src/lib/register-sw.ts, which refuses in dev/preview hosts to avoid stale previews.

- Keep shared local state in WorkshopProvider and navigation in WorkshopShell at the root; leaf routes render individual screens so navigation preserves loaded settings and orders.
- Use IndexedDB through SettingsRepository and OrderRepository; preserve order warranty snapshots when catalog entries change.
